# Figure Specifications

## Figure 1 — Dynamic Adjustment Mechanism (DAM) Formula

**Purpose:** canonical clause formula for reproduction in paper, presentations, and policy briefs.

Display elements:
1. WMVI definition.
2. Trigger-band breach definition.
3. Adjustment with cap/collar clipping.
4. Legend for each symbol.

Recommended layout:
- Panel A: $\text{WMVI}_t = \sum_k w_k z_{k,t}$
- Panel B: breach function $B_t$
- Panel C: adjustment $\Delta P_t = \operatorname{clip}(\gamma B_t P_t,-C^-_t,C^+_t)$

## Figure 2 — Risk Exposure Comparison (Headline)

**Purpose:** compare fixed-price, FIDIC 13.8 baseline, DAM.

Suggested plot design:
- X-axis: month (2015-01 to 2025-12)
- Y-axis: margin deviation or standardized risk exposure
- Three lines: Fixed-price, FIDIC 13.8, DAM
- Shaded windows: COVID and 2022 shock periods
- Side panel: annual employer exposure with cap line

## Figure 3 — Trigger-Performance Surface

Heatmap or contour:
- axes: lower/upper trigger settings (or equivalent symmetric threshold + cap)
- cell value: objective score (margin variance + penalties)
- overlay feasible region meeting employer/event constraints

## Figure 4 — Administrative Burden Frontier

Scatter/frontier plot:
- X-axis: adjustment event count
- Y-axis: margin variance
- points: candidate parameter sets
- mark selected DAM setting and continuous-indexation reference

## Figure 5 — Stress Scenario Cap Behaviour

Tail synthetic shocks:
- show unconstrained adjustment vs capped DAM adjustment
